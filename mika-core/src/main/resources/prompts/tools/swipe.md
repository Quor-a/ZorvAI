Swipe between two normalized 0-999 points. To scroll a page down, swipe upward (from a larger y to a smaller y). For ordinary scrolling use {{scroll}}. For a carousel, use a short sideways swipe; a long sideways swipe on a list row may delete it, so inspect the result. The result's effect says what the action did to the interface: done (a clear change), small_change (only focus, a toggle or one or two elements changed, or it may still be loading: check it), no_effect (nothing in the interface changed: try another way instead of repeating) or place_changed (now somewhere else, given in effect.to).
