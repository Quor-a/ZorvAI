Ask the vision model to read the whole screen for you, when you cannot tell from the element list what the page is or how it is laid out. Say in task what you are about to do. You get back what the page is, the controls you can use with their 0-999 centre points, anything odd or in the way, and hints for your task. It is slow (often over ten seconds): to find one control, icon or input box, use {{find_visually}} instead.
