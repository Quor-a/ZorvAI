Share a piece of text to an installed app by exact package name: opens that app's share screen on the phone's virtual display with the text filled in (an Android text share). Use it to send or post text into an app (a message to a contact, a note), especially when the app's screen cannot be read or typed into. It does not send anything: on the share screen, pick the recipient or destination and tap its send or confirm button yourself, then check the result. If the app cannot receive shared text, it returns cannot_share: open the app and type instead. If the system asks which one to use (an app installed twice, or several share targets), it returns ok:true with that question on screen: pick the option the task needs, and do not share again.
